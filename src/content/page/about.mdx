## Profile

British–Finnish artist working with abstract painting and material-led processes. Gough’s ongoing Wilderness in Paint series explores the relationship between natural forces and the painted surface. His developing series Matter uses cast iron powder, oxidised copper, crystallisation and efflorescence to investigate material transformation, time and agency.

## Professional experience & current research

Practising artist for over 20 years. Founding member and gallery manager, Block 336, Brixton, London (approximately 13 years).

2026 Matter: ongoing painting research with oxidised copper, cast iron powder, acrylic media and efflorescence.
